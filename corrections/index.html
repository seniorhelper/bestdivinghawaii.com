Corrections

What we got wrong, what we changed and when. A dated log of substantive fixes to our Hawaiʻi diving guides.

Best Diving Hawaiʻi · Updated October 2026 · How we research

This page lists substantive corrections to Best Diving Hawaiʻi: places where a fact was wrong, a rule had changed or an experience was misdescribed. We keep it so readers can see what changed and when. Small fixes, such as typos, broken links and formatting, are made without an entry here.

Corrections are made under our editorial policy. If you find something wrong, please tell us through the contact page, with the page address and, if you can, a link to the official source.

How We Handle a Reported Error

  1. Check the source. We look for the official or published source behind the claim: a DLNR rule, a county ordinance, a National Weather Service criterion, a National Park Service page or an established dive publication.
  2. Fix the page. If the page is wrong, we correct it, update the "Updated" date and adjust the sources list.
  3. Fix related pages. Many facts appear in more than one place on the site, such as a site's depth on an island page, in the Dive Site Finder and on the homepage. We search the site and correct every instance.
  4. Log it. Substantive corrections are added to the table above with the date.

What Counts as Substantive

  • A wrong location, depth range, entry type or certification level for a dive site.
  • A rule, law, closure or reservation requirement that is wrong or has changed.
  • An experience described in a way that would mislead someone booking it.
  • A claim that could not be supported by a source when we checked.

Opinions, such as which site we think suits a beginner, are not corrections, but we are always glad to hear a well-argued case.
