Who is planning a Hawaii dive

Hawaii welcomed 9.69 million visitors in 2024, and Oʻahu alone counted 5.79 million arriving by air. Maui, Kona and Kauaʻi each draw divers who book shops weeks ahead.

Divers are careful buyers: they compare certification options, boat size and site lists. A Featured listing that answers those questions and links straight to booking is the kind they click.

Questions owners ask

Is there a free option?

Yes. A basic listing on Best Diving Hawaii is free for dive shops, charters and snorkel operators serving the Hawaiian Islands. We verify every operator first. Featured is an optional upgrade at $29 a month.

What exactly do I get with Featured?

A top spot in the island, dive-site and dive-shop pages that match where you run trips, a Featured badge, one photo, a short pitch in your words and a direct link to your booking page. You can swap the photo or pitch whenever your season or offer changes.

Will Featured guarantee bookings?

No. We promise the placement and the work behind it, not a number of bookings. Visitors still decide based on your reviews, price, safety record and how easy you are to book.

How is a paid spot labeled?

Every paid placement shows a visible Featured badge, and the link to your site is marked rel="sponsored". That keeps the guide honest for readers and within search engine rules, and it protects your own site too.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime online. Your spot stays live through the month you’ve paid for and then returns to a basic listing.
