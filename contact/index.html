Before you write

We are a publisher, not a dive operator, so we cannot book dives, check availability or give personal dive or medical advice. For bookings, contact a dive shop directly. For current ocean conditions, check with lifeguards and the National Weather Service.

Reporting a mistake? Tell us which page it is on and, ideally, where the correct information is published.

What to Include

The more specific your message, the faster we can act on it.

  • Corrections: the page address, the sentence or table row that is wrong, and a link to the official or published source that shows the correct information. For rules and closures, a link to the DLNR, county, NOAA or National Park Service page is ideal.
  • Site changes: if a dive site has new access rules, parking changes, a closure or new hazards, tell us the date you saw it and what changed.
  • Dive operators: if your business is listed and the details are out of date, write from an address at your business domain and tell us what to update. Listings are descriptive and free; we do not sell rankings.
  • Advertising: see the advertise page. Paid placements are labeled and have no say over our safety, law or etiquette content.

What Happens Next

Factual errors about safety, rules or protected areas are checked against the source first and corrected as a priority. When we change something because a reader told us, the change is logged on our corrections page. We cannot reply to every general question, and we do not give personal dive-planning or medical advice.

Urgent? Do Not Use This Form

This form is not monitored around the clock. For anything urgent, contact the right agency directly:

  • Emergency on or in the water: call 911.
  • Injured, stranded or entangled turtle, monk seal, dolphin or whale: the statewide marine wildlife hotline, (888) 256-9840.
  • Fishing or collecting in a Marine Life Conservation District: the DLNR enforcement (DOCARE) hotline, 643-DLNR (643-3567), or the DLNRTip app.
  • Today's surf and beach hazards: the National Weather Service in Honolulu and Hawaii Beach Safety.
